How it works

Follow these simple steps to start using Petal-PH.

  1. Visit Login: Click the Login button and choose Sign Up if you're new.
  2. Create an account: Enter your email, nickname, status (student or graduated), anniversary, phone number, location, and password.
  3. Log in: Use your email and password to access your dashboard. Admins can log in with the admin account.
  4. Browse products: Explore seasonal flowers and add your chosen items to an order.
  5. Place and pay: Confirm your order and select a payment method: COD, GCash, Maya, or bank.
  6. Track and manage: Track orders, update your profile, and receive anniversary notifications.
